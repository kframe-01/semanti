# Verification Criterion

## Definition

A condition or measurable expected result derived from a Test Intent that provides a basis for checking whether the intended behavior was met.

## Usage

Reviewers identify observable results in the Test Intent before refining its verification criteria.

## Boundaries

- Includes: What must be observable or measurable to check the intent.
- Does not include: A recorded result from a particular execution.
