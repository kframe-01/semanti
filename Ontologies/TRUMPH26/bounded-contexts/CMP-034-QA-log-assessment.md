# Log Assessment

## Definition

A recorded judgment about whether an execution log is consistent with a specific Test Intent version and contains enough relevant information to assess the execution.

## Usage

A Maintenance Technician records a Log Assessment as consistent, incomplete, or inconsistent, with supporting observations.

## Boundaries

- Includes: A review tied to the intent version, execution, and log.
- Does not include: Automatically produced log events or an automatic certification of product conformity.
